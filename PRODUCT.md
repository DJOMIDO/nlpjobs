# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People exploring natural language processing job opportunities, especially
students, engineers, researchers, and practitioners who need to scan openings
and narrow them by location or urgency.

## Product Purpose

NLP Jobs is a focused job board for discovering NLP-related roles. Success
means a visitor can quickly understand the available opportunities, filter the
list, and open a job to inspect its details.

## Positioning

The product focuses specifically on natural language processing roles instead
of presenting a broad, noisy general-purpose job board.

## Operating Context

Visitors use the site in a browser to search, filter, compare, and inspect job
listings. The site is a lightweight portfolio project deployed on Netlify.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Browse jobs and open a detail page.
- Search by keyword.
- Filter by country, city, and urgent openings.
- Navigate paginated results.
- Data is served through a Netlify Function, with MongoDB optional and a
  committed demo dataset as fallback.
- Preserve the existing routes and job data shape.

## Brand Commitments

The existing `nlp.jobs` logo and the concise, practical job-board voice should
remain recognizable. The interface should feel useful before it feels
decorative.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains real job listing fields, logo assets, background SVGs,
and the deployed Netlify Function. No testimonials, customer logos, or
commercial claims should be invented.

## Product Principles

- Make relevant jobs easy to scan.
- Keep filtering and navigation obvious.
- Show useful job facts before decorative treatment.
- Preserve a lightweight, dependable experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Controls should be keyboard reachable, visibly focused, semantically labeled,
and usable across desktop and mobile widths.
